How to play

  • Travel the road. Each territory of the knowledge map teaches one part of music. Clear a level to claim one of its provinces; clear them all to conquer the territory and unlock the next.
  • Play with anything. The on-screen piano (on a tablet or phone, tap the keys and use several fingers for chords; in the Practice Room, ◀ ▶ change the octave), a MIDI keyboard (Chrome or Edge), your computer keyboard (Z–M and Q–U are two octaves, black keys on the row above, ↑ ↓ shift octave) or the mouse.
  • Challenges. Lessons teach; note and chord steps check you can find things; progressions use Roman numerals; rushes drop notes onto the keys in time; ear training asks what you hear; quizzes test the theory.
  • Stars. 60% accuracy clears a level, 80% earns two stars, 95% three. Three stars on some levels earn composer cards.
  • Collect. Codex pages, 20 composer cards, territory crests, 32 badges, ranks from Novice to Legend, and keyboards, note trails and banners from the shop, paid in ducats.
  • Practise daily. A fresh Daily Practice every day; keep a streak for bigger rewards. The Practice Room names any chord you play.
