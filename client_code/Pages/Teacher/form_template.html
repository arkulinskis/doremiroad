How it works

  1. You make a link on this page and text, email or post it to your student (or their parent).
  2. They open it and play. The checklist lives in their browser and ticks itself when a song or level is cleared. The minutes they play are counted for real, so there's no log to fill in.
  3. They send their week back. On "My practice week" they press Send to my teacher, or print one page with a line for a parent's initials. The report is a link too: open it and you see their minutes per day, what they cleared and the homework ticks.

Nothing about your students is stored on Doremi Road's server: the homework and the report both travel inside their links. Doremi Road is a practice companion, not a replacement for your lessons.
